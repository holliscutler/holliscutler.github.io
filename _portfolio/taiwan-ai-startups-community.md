**Co-founder and lead developer** · October 2025 – present ·
[taiwanaistartups.com](http://www.taiwanaistartups.com)

TASC connects the people shaping the future of artificial intelligence in
Taiwan: founders, builders, researchers, investors, and anyone else working in
the space. We program and host in-person talks, panels, workshops, and
networking events — past sessions have covered AI in education, embedded AI,
legal compliance for AI products, and how teams actually adopt AI in practice.

The community runs on three principles: gather in person, because that's where
the conversations that matter happen; invite speakers with practical experience
rather than theory; and keep participation cross-disciplinary across every role
in the ecosystem.

I built and maintain the community's website, which serves as its public home —
the events listing, the Substack newsletter signup, and the social channels
members find us through.
